THE ORIGINAL GAME

Hide together. Read together.

HIDE

Make six hands look convincing.

Two teams of three face each other. The hiding side disguises the transfer of the gol and conceals it in one player’s fist. Hand movements and misdirection give the guessing side competing clues.

FIND

Read the team. Commit to a hand.

The opposing team discusses the performance, identifies likely empty hands, and chooses where the gol is hidden. The reveal settles the round under the agreed scoring rules.

Agree on the scoring before the match.

Traditional variations use different point targets, empty-hand allowances, direct-guess bonuses, and Shah Gol rules. This group confirmed the original 3v3 format. Its exact scoring conventions have yet to be documented here.
