THE HOUSE FORMAT

How freestyle works.

One gol. Three rivals.

A randomly chosen player starts with the gol. After their performance, the other two players each choose a hand. The combination of guesses determines the next duel, the next holder, and whether anyone scores. First to 11 wins.

POINT + POSSESSION

The performer scores.

Both challengers miss. The performer earns one point and keeps the gol for the next performance.

Possession is the opportunity.

Winning a duel between the two correct challengers transfers the gol to the winner. That duel awards zero points. The new holder gets the next chance to perform and score.

The split-choice duel

When guesses differ, the correct challenger duels the performer. A performer victory earns one point and retains possession. A challenger victory transfers possession with zero points awarded.

The three-way rules come from the players. The precise procedure inside a duel has yet to be documented.
